INCIDENT REPORT
Case R2-16 | Incident: Transit Buffer Emergency Flush

WHAT HAPPENED
The transit buffer was flushed outside its maintenance window. The effect spread across the timeline network.

TIMELINE OF EVENTS
TIME     | EVENT
---------+---------------------------------------------------------
23:27:10 | First abnormal reading: transit buffer overrun of 222 MB
23:27:27 | Monitoring reports a sustained inconsistency
23:28:05 | Automatic recovery fails to restore normal readings

WHERE IT STARTED
The first abnormal reading came from Transit Buffer TB-2.
It lines up with Change #4915 made at 23:27:00 (forced flush of the transit buffer).
No other change in this period touched the transit buffer content.

OTHER NOTES
Readings before the incident were in their normal range.
Operators on shift reported no scheduled maintenance for this system.

END OF REPORT
