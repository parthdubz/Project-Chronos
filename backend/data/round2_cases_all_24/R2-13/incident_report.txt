INCIDENT REPORT
Case R2-13 | Incident: Anchor Relay Offset Change

WHAT HAPPENED
The anchor offsets were changed by hand. The effect spread across the timeline network.

TIMELINE OF EVENTS
TIME     | EVENT
---------+---------------------------------------------------------------------
20:03:01 | First abnormal reading: anchor drift of 370 ms along the relay chain
20:03:18 | Monitoring reports a sustained inconsistency
20:03:57 | Automatic recovery fails to restore normal readings

WHERE IT STARTED
The first abnormal reading came from Anchor Relay AR-7.
It lines up with Change #4560 made at 20:02:54 (manual change of the anchor offsets).
No other change in this period touched the anchor offset table.

OTHER NOTES
Readings before the incident were in their normal range.
Operators on shift reported no scheduled maintenance for this system.

END OF REPORT
