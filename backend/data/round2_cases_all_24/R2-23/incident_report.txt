INCIDENT REPORT
Case R2-23 | Incident: Cross-Timeline Sync Shutdown

WHAT HAPPENED
The cross-timeline sync controller was stopped. The effect spread across the timeline network.

TIMELINE OF EVENTS
TIME     | EVENT
---------+---------------------------------------------------------
21:22:31 | First abnormal reading: 10 timeline channels out of sync
21:22:53 | Monitoring reports a sustained inconsistency
21:23:36 | Automatic recovery fails to restore normal readings

WHERE IT STARTED
The first abnormal reading cannot be traced to a single source. Possible terminals: Sync Controller SG-2 and Sync Controller SG-5.
No change record could be matched to the reading with certainty.

OTHER NOTES
Readings before the incident were in their normal range.
Operators on shift reported no scheduled maintenance for this system.

END OF REPORT
